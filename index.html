Dingyuan Huang

My research connects robot perception, manipulation, human–robot interaction, and embodied intelligence. As a Ph.D. researcher at the University of Florida, I develop robotic systems that translate visual observations into physical actions, with applications in medical assistance and intelligent manufacturing. My work also includes humanoid robot learning.

Research Focus

Visual Perception for Robotic Action

Connecting visual recognition with physical action through 3D target localization, grasp guidance, and action verification. This work builds on experience in object recognition and medical image classification.

Sensor-Guided Manipulation

Combining motion planning, end-effector control, and force and tactile feedback for physical manipulation. My work spans modular robot platforms, teleoperation, and the integration of perception with hardware execution.

Humanoid Learning & Embodied Intelligence

Exploring vision–language–action models, imitation learning, and reinforcement learning for humanoid robot behavior. Contributing as a co-author to JIC 2026 and HOIST 2026 research in this area.
